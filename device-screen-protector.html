3 Easy Steps To Apply

01

Clean The Screen Thoroughly

Wipe the device screen with a microfiber cloth and alcohol solution to remove dust, fingerprints, and oils. Make sure the surface is completely clean and dry.

02

Apply the Spray Coating

Spray a light, even layer of the nanotechnology solution onto the screen (or onto the cloth if required). Gently spread it across the entire surface using a clean microfiber cloth.

03

Let It Dry and Cure

Allow the coating to dry for a few minutes, then leave the device untouched for the recommended curing time (usually 1–2 hours). Once cured, the screen is protected and ready for use.
